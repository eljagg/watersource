Privacy notice

Who we are. The Water Resources Authority (WRA) is the data controller for personal data processed in WaterSource Jamaica. Contact the WRA Data Protection Officer at dpo@wra.gov.jm (to be confirmed by WRA).

What we collect and why. Name, address, email and telephone number of applicants and data submitters, collected to process water abstraction licence applications and periodic data submissions under the Water Resources Act. The lawful basis is the performance of WRA's statutory functions.

Who sees it. WRA staff involved in reviewing and approving applications and submissions. Personal data is never published; only approved data classified as public appears in public views, exports and dashboards.

How long we keep it. Licence records for the life of the licence plus seven years; inactive client accounts are anonymised after 24 months of inactivity; refused applications after three years. These periods are confirmed with WRA's Data Protection Officer.

Your rights. You may access the personal data we hold (download it from your profile), ask for it to be corrected, object to processing and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Requests are answered within 30 days. You may complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner.

Security. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and logged, and the system is hosted on WRA's own servers in Jamaica.
