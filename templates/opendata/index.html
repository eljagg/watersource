For developers

Every dataset has a stable CSV address (and GeoJSON where it is spatial), for example /open-data/monitoring-sites.csv. Map layers are at /maps/layers/<parishes|basins|wmus|aquifers|sites>.geojson. The full programming interface, with authentication for non-public data, is documented at API documentation.

Terms of use

You may copy, publish, adapt and combine this data for any purpose, provided you attribute it to the Water Resources Authority of Jamaica and the date you obtained it, and do not suggest WRA endorses your use. The data is provided as is; WRA does not warrant its completeness or fitness for any purpose. Personal data is processed under the Data Protection Act, 2020 and is never released here.
